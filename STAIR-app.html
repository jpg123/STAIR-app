Clinical Research Disclosure

STAIR is a planning tool derived from a Canadian provincial registry review of burns, frostbite, and necrotizing fasciitis admissions spanning 2004-2024.

The app summarizes comparative resource intensity for institutional planning and funding discussions. It is a research and administrative aid, not a bedside decision tool.

The underlying analysis uses frozen cohort factors from an offline STAIR resource-intensity derivation to communicate differences in hospital stay, ICU use, procedures, transfusion burden and related resource domains across the three diagnosis groups. The app does not re-estimate weights or calculate patient-level predictions.

No patient-identifiable data is entered or transmitted from this device.

Derivation and scoring details

STAIR has two separate steps. First, pooled FAMD was fit offline to 8 resource variables. The first dimension summarizes shared resource-intensity patterns; the normalized absolute first-dimension loadings are shown below. Second, each diagnosis factor is the mean shifted FAMD score for that cohort divided by the mean Burn score. The app applies only those frozen cohort factors to entered case counts and does not calculate patient-level scores.
